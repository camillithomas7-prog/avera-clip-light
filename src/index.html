FAQ

Good questions.

How does the translation work?

Download the free companion app shown in the guide (iPhone and Android) and connect the earbuds. The app uses your phone to recognise and translate speech in 132 languages and plays the translation in your ear. It needs a mobile data or Wi-Fi connection. Music and calls work like any Bluetooth earbuds, no app needed.

Is AVERA Clip a hearing aid?

No, it is not a medical device. Voice boost mode works with sound amplifier apps (such as Google Sound Amplifier or the equivalent on iPhone): your phone picks up the sound around you and plays it louder and clearer in your earbuds. It helps with conversations and talks, but it does not replace a prescribed hearing aid.

Does it sound good if nothing goes in my ear?

Yes. The capsule points towards your ear canal and delivers sound straight to your eardrum. As they are open-ear, people very close to you may hear a faint sound at very high volume in a quiet room.

Will they stay on while I run?

The flexible clip hooks onto the edge of your ear and fits all ear sizes. It stays secure while running, cycling, at the gym and through sudden moves.

Will they work with my phone?

They work with any Bluetooth smartphone, tablet or computer: iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows.

How fast is delivery?

We dispatch within 24 working hours with tracked delivery. UK orders usually arrive in 3–6 working days, free of charge.

What if they're not for me?

You have 30 days to send them back for a full refund. Every AVERA Clip also comes with a 1-year warranty.
